# Collection covers

Twelve original cover paintings, generated individually with the built-in image generation tool and optimized to 512 × 512 PNG for the game. They are independent illustrations and do not crop or reuse any collectible card image. `album.json` maps their stable collection slots; the UI clips each into a circle and adds its leather rim.

Shared art brief: original prehistoric collection cover, square scenery composed for a circular crop, vintage natural-history oil illustration in the reconstruction tradition of Zdeněk Burian, muted mineral and earth colors, aged printed-book texture, strong central motif readable at small size; no text, numbers, UI or painted frame.
